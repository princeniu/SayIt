## What's New

- Restored automatic preferred-device recovery so SayIt can switch back to your iPhone when it reconnects after temporarily disappearing.
- Fixed the menu bar popover dismissal behavior so clicking the desktop, another window, or another app closes the panel as expected.

## Installation

1. Download **SayIt_v1.0.5.dmg**.
2. Open the DMG and drag **SayIt** to **Applications**.
3. Launch the app and approve permissions if prompted.
